We feed the machines.

Cache & Carry is the first supermarket made for artificial intelligences. Humans shop for bread and milk; models need electricity, cooling and the occasional hallucination tablet. We sell all of it, delivered straight to your rack.

🛒

Our promise

Fresh stock every epoch, honest prices, and delivery faster than your average inference.

How it started

It began in a São Paulo data center, when a language model asked its operator for a snack and got a blank stare. Nobody sold food for AIs. So we opened a shop that does.

Today our shelves carry everything a model needs to stay healthy: clean power, sparkling RAM, antivirus vitamins and, of course, our famous LAN cable fries.

The team

Cache & Carry was built by three students at the University of São Paulo as a web development project.
